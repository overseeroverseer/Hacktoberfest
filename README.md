# Purdue's 2026 Hacktoberfest

## SafeBytes 🍽️🛡️ (PurePlate? or MenuGuard?)

Dining out should not be an interrogation. SafeBytes provides a solution: Real-time allergen awareness and cross-contamination alerts for peace of mind at the table.

## 📖 The Spark (Our Story)
Dining out with friends should be about conversation and good food. For our friend group, it often starts with an interrogation:
*"Does this sauce contain peanut oil? Is the fryer shared with shellfish?"*

Watching our close friend navigate severe allergies showed us that dining out is loaded with anxiety. Waitstaff can be uninformed, menus lack granular ingredient flags, and hidden cross-contamination risks turn a casual meal into an emergency room hazard.

We built **SafeBytes** to eliminate the guesswork. By bridging transparent restaurant menu data with proactive consumer warnings, we ensure nobody at the table has to wonder if their next bite is safe.

### ⚙️ What It Does

#### 1. For Restaurants (Data Ingestion & Tagging)
- **Menu Management Portal:** Kitchens input dishes as well as their potential allergens.
- **Allergen Flagging:** Uses structured dietary datasets (e.g., FDA Top 9 allergens, USDA food databases) to cross-reference common allergens.

#### 2. For Diners (Proactive Alerts)
- **Pre-Order Warning System:** Scans the selected meal, flagging common allergens before they place an order.
